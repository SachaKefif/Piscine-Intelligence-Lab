## IB4003 — 2004-11-02

Manual transcript: [transcript_IB4003.txt](transcript_IB4003.txt)

### abstract

- IB4003.rdhillon.s.1: The project manager opened the meeting and described the layout of the new location the group might be moving to.
- IB4003.rdhillon.s.2: The project manager answered questions regarding the number of people per office and whether a lounge was necessary.

- IB4003.rdhillon.s.12: The team will merge their proposal on the arrangement of people in the space with that of a different team.
- IB4003.rdhillon.s.13: One team member will ask Maggie and Pierrette about their preferences on the arrangement of people.
- IB4003.rdhillon.s.14: The team will meet next Tuesday at two o'clock
